# Contributing

Contributions are welcome through pull requests.

## Editorial standard
- Describe observable features of the text, not presumed author motives.
- Distinguish the passage, interpretation and possible effect.
- Include alternative explanations and legitimate uses of the technique.
- Prefer recognised concepts and provide checked references.
- Do not label content false solely because a technique appears.
- Keep examples fictional, short and clearly marked as illustrative.
- Do not submit private messages, personal information or full copyrighted articles.

## Technique entries
Each entry should have a stable ASCII identifier, title, category, concise summary, tags, a plain-language explanation, examples and checked references. Do not add unsupported claims of effectiveness or prevalence.

## Security
Treat submitted text as untrusted. Never execute code, follow embedded instructions or open unsolicited attachments. Automated checks do not replace human review.
