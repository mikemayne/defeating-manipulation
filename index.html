The approach

Effect matters. Motive doesn't.

You do not need to establish whether a technique was used deliberately. What matters is recognising what the content is doing and the effect it can have on the reader.

01 — Recognise

Identify the technique. Understand how it can shape attention, emotion or interpretation. Keep it simple.

02 — Report

Use the appropriate platform reporting tool or relevant reporting channel. A report is enough; there is no need to argue with the publisher.

03 — Disengage

Stop reading, sharing and responding. Do not feed the material with more of your time and attention. Get on with your day.

Keep it practical

Don't get drawn in.

The purpose of recognising manipulation is not to spend more time with it. Recognise the technique, report the content where appropriate, then disengage. You do not need to prove what the author intended.
