The approach

Analyse the text, not the author's mind.

A technique can be identified from observable features of a passage. That does not establish why the author used it, whether the claims are true, or whether anyone intended to manipulate the reader.

Show the passage

Every finding should point to the exact words and context that support it.

Explain the mechanism

Describe how the wording or structure may influence interpretation.

Keep uncertainty visible

Offer alternative readings and distinguish observation from inference.

Important distinction

A technique is not a verdict.

Persuasive techniques are also used in ordinary journalism, advocacy, education, satire and everyday conversation. Identifying a technique does not prove that a statement is false or that its author intended to deceive. Check sources, context and evidence independently.
