# Mapping the Surviving a Psyop catalogue to online article patterns

This document records the review of the pattern chapters in [surviving-a-psyop](https://github.com/mikemayne/surviving-a-psyop). It covers the chapters under *Recognising manipulation*, *Intimidation & pressure* and *Technology & perception*.

The question is whether a comparable pattern can be identified in the words of an online article. A direct or partial match is mapped to a technique ID in this repository. Other chapters are retained in the inventory with an explanation of why they are not standalone article-text techniques.

The mapping does not transfer assumptions about how or why an event described in the source guide occurred.

## Existing catalogue entries

The following source patterns overlap with the initial catalogue and do not need duplicate entries:

- Loaded language and moral accusations: `loaded_language`.
- Personal attacks and negging: `ad_hominem`.
- Embedded accusations and loaded questions: `presupposition`.
- Popularity and apparent group agreement: `bandwagon`.
- Selective evidence and context removal: `selective_presentation` and `out_of_context_quote`.
- Repetition and priming: `repetition` and `priming`.
- Fear appeals and constrained choices: `appeal_to_fear` and `false_dilemma`.
- Simplified blame and responsibility: `scapegoating`.
- Unsupported causal chains: `slippery_slope`.

## Research basis

The expanded catalogue uses established work on propaganda detection, online news framing and rhetorical techniques. The research supports identifying specific textual spans and explaining the technique. More specific labels in this mapping are descriptive extensions where a source-guide pattern has a clear online-language counterpart.
